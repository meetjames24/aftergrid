Listen first

Anyone may listen on any of these. Transmitting on amateur (ham) bands needs a licence except in a genuine life-threatening emergency where no other means exist; licence-free radios (PMR446, FRS, CB) can be used by anyone. Rules differ by country: check yours. Print this page (Ctrl+P) and keep it with your radios.

Tools on this Aftergrid

ProgramCHIRP: read and program Baofeng and most handheld radios over a USB cable.
MeshMesh Messenger (NomadNet / Reticulum): encrypted text messages and pages between Aftergrid machines over Wi-Fi, LoRa or radio, with no internet.
MeshMeshtastic (terminal: meshtastic): configure and message through Meshtastic LoRa radios.
HFJS8Call and fldigi: keyboard messaging over HF radio, works at very weak signals.
EmailPat (Winlink): send and receive email over radio. Needs a ham licence and callsign.
PacketDirewolf: software TNC for APRS and packet radio.
SDRGqrx: listen to any frequency with a cheap USB RTL-SDR dongle. rtl_433: decode weather stations and sensors. Gpredict: track weather and amateur satellites.
